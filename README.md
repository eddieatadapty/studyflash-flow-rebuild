# Studyflash Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/studyflash-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Studyflash users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Studyflash's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Let them study one card from their own lecture before the price | +10–25% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Turn the exam date into the plan, and the plan into the paywall | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Make the yearly saving impossible to miss | +10–20% ARPU |
| 04 | Put your proof on the screen everyone watches | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 05 | Offer the semester plan to students who close the paywall | +5–10% CR to purchase |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Studyflash's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Studyflash. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
